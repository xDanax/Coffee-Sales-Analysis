## 🎯 Objective
Identify which customer segments, product types, and regions drive the most revenue in order to optimize product offerings, loyalty and marketing strategies.

### Key business questions:
- `Which roast types and package sizes generate the highest sales, and which underperform?`
- `Which countries should the business prioritize for growth and marketing investment?`
- `Is the loyalty program effective in increasing customer spend?`

### Table 1: Sizes x Roast Type

- Light Roast dominate total sales, bringing in ~$17.3k in revenue across all sizes while Dark and Medium generate ~$13.2k and ~$14.6k respectively.

- Larger sizes generate higher revenue per transaction
   - 2.5kg alone generates $23,785, which is ~52% of total sales ($23,785 out of ~$45,159)
   - 1kg + 2.5kg combined generate ~$34,800, or ~77% of total revenue
   - Small sizes (0.2kg and 0.5kg) contribute relatively little in comparison

- The loyalty program appears effective in Ireland and ineffective in the UK and US.


## 📝 Recommendations

1. Reassess the loyalty program and make country-specific adjustments as it is not yet driving higher total sales as a one-size fits all strategy.

2. Use small sizes as conversion tools
   - Offer discounts on 0.2kg / 0.5kg sizes for first-time buyers
   - Use follow-up incentives to convert them to 1kg+ purchases

3. Focus promotions, inventory, and pricing optimization on bulk-packages (large sizes) and Light Roast coffee as any disruption to these categories would significantly impact revenue.

4. Prioritize pricing, inventory and loyalty strategies in the US as this will have the largest revenue impact.


## 🚀 Expected Impact

These recommendations are expected to:
- Maximizes revenue from the segments already driving most sales
- Improves funnel efficiency from trial → repeat bulk purchase
- Create a loyalty program that drives sales
